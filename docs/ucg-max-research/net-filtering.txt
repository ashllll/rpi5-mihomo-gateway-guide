Filtering Syntax
Filtering follows a structured, URL-safe syntax with three types of expressions.
1. Property Expressions
<property>.<function>(<arguments>)
Examples:
id.eq(123)
id
123
name.isNotNull()

createdAt.in(2025-01-01, 2025-01-05)

2. Compound Expressions
<logical-operator>(<expressions>)
Examples:
and(name.isNull(), createdAt.gt(2025-01-01))

3. Negation Expressions
not(<expression>)
Example:
not(name.like('guest*'))
